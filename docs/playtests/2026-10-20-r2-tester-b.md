## Best, worst and willingness to return

**Best:** Aylin needed flour for a school kitchen, and telling her what I witnessed made her put the shopping bag away. I understood that consequence immediately. Learning Kemal was Rıza’s cousin made the story feel connected to the marriage. Seeing Kemal help Safiye also made him more interesting than a simple cheat.

**Worst:** After I called Rıza stingy to his daughter, she said “I’ve made up my mind.” I could not tell what mind she had made up. After I vouched for Emre, Rıza said “I have Elif to think about.” I could see the spoken words, but could not tell whether the marriage had moved closer. I am not claiming those actions had no effect.

**Boredom:** At 02:07 I chose Gallop because nobody was nearby and I wanted something to happen. **Frustration:** The crowded phone screen hid replies by 02:39, and by 03:41 that was a reason to leave. **Quit:** At 03:59, voluntarily, with the marriage still considering and no clear sense of progress. I would not return voluntarily as this player. The budget did not expire.
