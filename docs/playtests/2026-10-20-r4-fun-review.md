**Verdict:** After Day 1, I wanted to continue to see whether Cem's testimony reached Rıza. It did. ## 1. The three biggest reasons to stop

### 1. Conversations become a form before they become relationships

**Moment:** Day 1, 16:20–16:30. I told Cem that Emre keeps promises, tells the truth, and treats people well. Each required reopening the conversation and selecting a trait. Each got belief, trust, and a promise to pass it on. By the third endorsement I was completing three requirements, not persuading a neighbour. [Screens 032–040](../../scratch/r4/shots-fun/032.png).

Day 2 made this worse. At 06:16 Zehra accepted my deliberate lie that Murat is dishonest. At 13:37 Cem accepted my lie that Murat breaks promises. The response had essentially the same structure as the truthful endorsements. I chose an accusation, but did not invent a plausible incident, answer a suspicious question, or choose a concession.

**Why I stop:** Once I learn which listener accepts my word, the next interaction promises another selection from the same form. The trait system supports many claims, but those claims do not yet produce enough different decisions. More names and alternate sentence openings will not fix that.

### 2. Following one plan requires catching a timetable and recovering buried context

**Moment:** Day 1, 06:49. I was answering Aylin's specific school-flour question. The phone screenshot showed a grid of more than twenty names occupying most of the modal, with the concrete flour account below the visible scroll area. The harness let me tap named buttons outside that area. My named-button taps therefore understate the scrolling needed to keep the live question in view. [Screen 007](../../scratch/r4/shots-fun/007.png).

On Day 2 I saw Rıza available at 12:27. By my next attempt he was gone; at 13:12 only Cem remained. I also let Ali's 06:45 question pass while waiting, then found Emre's overpayment incident in the feed and conversation menu. These are not bug reports. They show how easily a plan becomes searching lists, watching time, and recovering context. My own reading and command delays contributed to the missed visits.

**Why I stop:** I want to decide who should hear something. Instead I spend attention finding the relevant person and separating their story from repeated claims that unrelated neighbours keep promises. The clock supplies urgency without making the competing uses of my time equally clear.

### 3. Exposure ends with a penalty where I want a decision

**Moment:** Day 2, 18:59. Rıza was cool and the benches were empty. The latest feed entries were short refusals, including “They should be here for this.” At 23:00 the summary recovered the important scene: Rıza had confronted me because my account did not hold up, and the benches would stay empty for a day. [Screens 082](../../scratch/r4/shots-fun/082.png), [083](../../scratch/r4/shots-fun/083.png).

I had a reason to worry: I had told both Cem and my household member Osman that Rıza was cruel. Both said they would buy bread elsewhere. Osman then passed Rıza without greeting him. But the exposure did not present a decision inside that confrontation. I tried to reach Rıza afterward to make amends; he had left. That was not a test of correcting Cem or Osman, the people I had misled about him. I did not successfully test Make amends.

**Why I stop:** The game demonstrated that my words hurt people, then announced a closure penalty. I wanted the confrontation itself to make me choose: own the lie, retract it in front of those I misled, or risk defending it. Empty benches make failure visible, but the scene did not make the next repair action clear to me. I stopped before Day 3; I am not claiming that the penalty disables conversation or that repair is absent.

## 3. What repeats

- **The action sequence:** open listener → select target → select trait → Tell → reopen. I repeated it three times on Cem just to cover Emre's three virtues.
- **The response:** a different opening, then belief or doubt, then “I'll pass on…” or “I won't repeat…”. Truth and fabrication often feel alike at the moment of speech.
- **The background claims:** many people keep promises; echoes report more speakers and counts. These establish circulation but repeatedly compete with the one relationship I am trying to follow.
- **The social consequences:** buying elsewhere and withholding a greeting are legible once. Repetition gives me less reason to care unless the affected person demands something new from me.
- **The evening invitation:** both summaries promised the Rıza–Cem dusk meeting. Night 1 connected directly to my plan. Night 2 repeated the meeting while asking the broader “Will someone see enough tomorrow to settle the doubt?”

## 4. Is there a reason to return tomorrow?

**Yes, but it is narrow.** Day 1's specific dusk appointment made me want to press Sleep. On Day 2 the honest account actually reached Rıza. That resolves the question the game planted. The Day 4 coffee appointment supplies a further deadline, but I did not reach it.

The hook weakens when tomorrow means another incident, another endorsement, and another wait for the same meeting. After exposure, my strongest question was how to repair the damage. The next-day prompt did not name the existing retraction action or a person to approach.

**The single strongest addition: a scheduled verification that gives me the first move tomorrow.** For example, after my lie about Murat breaking promises, someone tells me: “Murat is bringing the tools at opening. Cem will be here.” End the day on whether I will correct Cem before Murat arrives. Start the next morning with Cem reachable and that decision still pending. Retraction, warning Murat, or doubling down should produce different encounters. This is one proposed scene, not something observed in this build or an appointment to repeat every night. Reveal only arrangements Hatice has heard about. The reason to return is an unfinished decision caused by my own words.

## What the source check established afterward

The consequences I felt are intentional. `docs/design.md` explicitly specifies shop avoidance, changed greetings, confrontation, and a day of empty benches. It also says the full rumour route stays hidden until the fortnight ends. I am asking for better access to witnessed developments, not removal of that information limit.

`src/sim/game.ts:288` (`reaction`) constructs the common belief/trust/next-action response, including Rıza's mother exception and shop avoidance. `game.ts:1288` explicitly stages the trusted-source dusk relay. `game.ts:139` onward settles wedding doubts from belief plus testimony or observation; endorsing three virtues to Cem does not itself guarantee all three reach Rıza. `game.ts:576` (`confrontHatice`) applies the closure and trust cost without an in-scene response choice. `game.ts:381` (`closeDay`) selects recent events for the summary and uses the dusk meeting as its default tomorrow line. `game.ts:941` already lets Hatice retract claims to the listener she misled. My criticism is that the confrontation did not lead me into that action, not that retraction needs to be invented. These explain the repeated structures; they do not make them entertaining.
