## Best, worst and leaving

**Best:** Seeing Promises tick because **Cem told Rıza**, rather than because I told Rıza myself. I understood a useful route through another person. The initial lie being believed was enjoyable too. It later getting caught made the people feel less like free buttons.

**Worst:** Asking Cem about my selected kindness topic and getting honesty plus “Doesn’t believe you.” I could not tell what my input had asked, or what that verdict referred to.

**Frustration first recorded:** 04:03, after missing Cem twice and waiting through a day without the kindness message landing. Tool interaction and GALLOP contributed to the missed taps; this is not evidence that a person using a real touchscreen could never catch him.

**Boredom / desire to leave:** At 05:16 I felt I was waiting on a repeated promise. At 05:25 the delayed coffee and another tomorrow-at-dusk notice made me leave. I did not finish the fortnight. I did not reach the budget.

**Am I getting anywhere?** Yes at 01:50, when the first tick appeared, and again when honesty settled. By the stop, I had two settled qualities and damaged trust. I could not see a concrete recovery step or whether the kindness message was progressing. That uncertainty ended the session.

**Would I open it tomorrow voluntarily?** No. A visible way to recover Rıza’s trust, plus some indication of whether my message is travelling or blocked, would give me a reason to return. This is a player preference, not a claim that the game has no recovery rule.
