## The playable objective

Hatice opens a paid workers’ lunch on Day 13 at 13:00. On Day 4 she pays 120 lira for a bread/produce sample, accepts a 150-lira credit invoice, or declines. On Day 8 she pays Aylin 180 lira for preparation with an explicit leaving time, puts the fee on the account (work waits for payment), or cancels. Four 100-lira meals cover the 380-lira paid-now lunch; the credit branch costs 410 and needs five. Tea customers fund the garden through actual visits and payments. No payment is inferred from a promise. Debt stays in the local chapter and must block unsupported departure.

Rıza delivers counted bread; he is not a baker. Osman supplies produce; his old tenant account is separate from the garden invoice. Aylin’s school shift and evening remain hers. Dilan and Ece may book a lunch stop during their own break. Nermin makes only her own reservation and does not promise agency clients. Derya’s morning sleep and night work remain constraints. No new foreground customer or couple is added.

Reservations depend on each customer’s received beliefs about Osman’s honesty, Aylin’s and Rıza’s reliability, Hatice’s kindness and trust in her word. Asking identifies the received source. Honest/kind/generous/reliable testimony can travel after completed HF chats. Each route keeps its parent/root. A witnessed contradiction recalibrates sources and can bring the target to confront Hatice. Owning it commits to correcting each listener separately. Standing by it closes the garden for a day. Avoidance changes actual locations, purchases and lunch attendance. Amends do not erase copies already passed on.

The visible opening states the lunch goal and Day 4/8/13 dates. The strip keeps supplier/cook/reservation progress available. The agreement panel shows cash, invoice and prices before a decision. Day 13 serves actual attending reservations; missing agreements cancel the lunch, inadequate custom makes a loss. The fortnight ending reports that result, not a confidence count.
